# Color Sudoku — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 2026_

**Overview.** Color Sudoku does not require an account and does not collect personal information itself.
Your games, settings and statistics are stored only on your device.

**Advertising.** The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob. Google may collect and use data such as
your device's advertising identifier (only if you allow tracking), IP address, approximate location, device
information and ad interactions, to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud. Where required by law (for
example in the EEA, UK and some US states) you are asked for consent first, and you can change your choice at
any time in Settings › Ad Privacy Choices. Whether apps may use your advertising identifier is controlled by
Apple's "Allow Apps to Request to Track" setting. See <https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads>.

**Purchases.** The optional "Remove Ads" purchase is processed by Apple. We receive no payment details.
After purchase the advertising SDK is not started at all.

**Children.** Color Sudoku is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

**Deleting your data.** All game data is stored on your device; deleting the app deletes it. For data held by
Google, see <https://policies.google.com/privacy>.

**Changes.** We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version.
